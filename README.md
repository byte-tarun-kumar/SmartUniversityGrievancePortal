# Smart University Grievance Portal

The **University Grievance Portal** is a secure, transparent, and efficient platform designed to help students, faculty, and staff submit and track grievances. The portal ensures that concerns are handled promptly while maintaining confidentiality and data security throughout the process.

## 🎯 Purpose

The portal aims to create a safe and responsive environment where every voice is heard, and grievances are addressed efficiently and fairly.

## ✨ Key Features

### 🔐 Secure Submission
All grievances are submitted through a secure platform that ensures complete confidentiality and data protection.

### 📊 Real-time Tracking
Users can track the status of their grievance in real time, from submission to final resolution.

### 🕵️ Confidentiality Options
Users may choose to remain anonymous or confidential, ensuring their privacy is respected throughout the grievance process.

### 🔔 Regular Updates
Timely notifications and updates keep users informed about progress and any actions taken on their grievance.

## 🛡️ Security & Privacy

- Encrypted data handling
- Role-based access control
- Confidential grievance management

## 🤝 Who Can Use It?

- Students  
- Faculty Members  
- Administrative Staff  

## 📌 Conclusion

The University Grievance Portal is committed to fostering trust, transparency, and accountability by ensuring that every concern is handled with care, confidentiality, and efficiency.
